About Us

Built for Somali Learners
Loogu Talagalay Barashada Soomaalida

Kulan Institute of Learning was founded with a single mission: to make high-quality technology education accessible to Somali-speaking communities worldwide. Machadka Barashada Kulan waxa la aasaasay hal ujeeddo: in waxbarashada tignoolajiya tayo sare leh loo fududeeyo bulshooyinka Soomaalida adduunka oo dhan.

We believe language should never be a barrier to learning. Our courses are bilingual, culturally relevant, and practically focused — giving learners the tools to thrive in the digital economy.

Accessible La Geli Karo
Content in both English and Somali, removing language barriers.
Practical Wax Ku Ool ah
Real-world skills you can apply immediately in your work.
Structured Habaysan
Well-organized modules with clear learning paths.
Community Bulshada
Learn alongside a growing Somali tech community.
