About KIST KIST Ku Saabsan

Built for a community the world overlooked.

Bulshada adduunku garan waayay ayaan u dhisay.

KIST is the technology and science education arm of Kulan Group Ltd — delivering courses in AI, cybersecurity, and digital skills fully in English and Somali to underserved communities worldwide.

KIST waa qaybta waxbarashada teknoolajiyada ee Kulan Group Ltd — oo bixisa koorsooyin AI, amniga xogta, iyo xirfadaha dhijitaalka ah oo buuxda oo Ingiriis iyo Soomaali ah.
