Our Mission

Education That Belongs to Everyone
Waxbarashada Dadka Oo Dhan

Kulan Institute was founded on a simple belief: language should never be a barrier to learning technology. We build every course in both English and Somali so our community can grow without leaving their identity at the door. Kulan Institute waxaa lagu aasaasay aaminsanaan fudud: luqaddu waa inayan noqon caqabad barashada teknoolojiyada.

🌎
Bilingual First — Labo Luqadood

Every lesson exists in both English and Somali — not translated, built bilingual from day one.

Cashar kasta wuxuu ku jiraa labadaba Ingiriisi iyo Soomaali.
